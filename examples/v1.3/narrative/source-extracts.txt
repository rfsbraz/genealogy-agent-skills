All people, places and records below are fictional training material, not real archival sources.

A birth register records Beatriz Mar, born 3 May 1884 in Vila Clara.

A 1907 marriage entry names Beatriz Mar, aged 25, seamstress, marrying Tomás Neri.

In 1910 Beatriz Mar and Tomás Neri appear at Rua do Poço 8 with a daughter, Rosa, aged two.

A grandson recalls that Beatriz taught neighbourhood children to sew. He does not know her death date.
