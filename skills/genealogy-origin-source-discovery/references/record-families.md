# Record-family investigation guide

Use this as a prompt to investigate local practice. It asserts no universal availability, contents or custody rule.

| Family | Questions and clues | Checks and pitfalls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Civil/religious life events | Parents, spouses, dates, residence, witnesses, marginal changes | Distinguish birth/baptism, marriage/licence, death/burial; inspect original and margins; registration coverage varies. |
| Census/household/population | Household, ages, occupation, residence, migration | Informant and household relationship labels may be uncertain; a shared address does not prove kinship. |
| Notarial/land/property/tax | Purchases, spouses, heirs, boundaries, occupation, wealth | Verify record creator and legal context; follow parcel and transaction chains; ownership is not residence. |
| Inheritance/probate/guardianship | Heirs, minors, relatives, debts, family disputes | Distinguish legal and biological relationships; omitted heirs require contextual analysis. |
| Courts/police | Identity details, witnesses, family events, addresses | Allegations and testimony need attribution; case outcome and access restrictions matter. |
| Military | Recruitment, age, birthplace, next of kin, service | Identify branch, period, unit and record custodian; do not infer a service history from one roster entry. |
| Migration/passport/consular | Origins, destinations, companions, citizenship | Names, ages and nationalities can vary; compare both sides of movement; inspect applications and supporting papers. |
| Newspapers/directories | Obituaries, notices, business, social events, addresses | OCR errors and reused reporting; distinguish same-name persons; publication date differs from event date. |
| Burial/cemetery | Death/burial, relatives, plot ownership | A memorial can be later than the event; a shared plot need not establish biological kinship. |
| School/work/hospital/charity | Admission, parents/guardians, occupation, movement | Institutions may retain records or transfer them; distinguish service users, staff and donors. |
| Private/family/religious-community | Letters, diaries, photographs, family books, oral testimony | Establish creator, date, custody, source dependence and sharing permission; retrospective accounts can be inaccurate. |
| Maps/gazetteers/local histories | Place identity, changing boundaries, contextual events | Establish period and scale; general context does not prove an individual's presence or experience. |

For every promising record family, formulate a discriminating question and verify a concrete collection before scheduling acquisition.
