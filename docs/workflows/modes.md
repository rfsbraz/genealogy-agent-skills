# Interview, investigation and story modes · v1.3

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Start or resume] --> B{What do you need?}
    B -->|Build a tree| C[Conversational interview]
    C --> D[Save testimony and provisional links]
    D --> E{Continue or research?}
    E -->|Next question| C
    E -->|Pause| F[Save checkpoint]
    E -->|Research| G[Research and GPS review]
    B -->|Investigate| G
    G --> H[Current evidence and conclusions]
    B -->|Tell a story| I[Read current person evidence]
    H --> I
    I --> J{Story style}
    J -->|Documentary or evocative| K[Records, memory, context and inference]
    J -->|Novelized| L[Add identified imagined scenes]
    K --> M[Story with evidence notes]
    L --> M
    M --> N[Save separately from evidence]
```

The user can switch modes at any time. Save the interview checkpoint before research or storytelling. Interview claims remain provisional until reviewed; a story can honestly describe records and recollections before a GPS conclusion exists. Imagined scenes stay outside the evidence graph. Contextual sources are consulted and cited for the story, not treated as proof of individual participation.
